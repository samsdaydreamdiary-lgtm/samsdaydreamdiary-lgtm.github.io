Privacy Policy

Sam's Daydream Uploader

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This tool is used only by the owner of the "Sam's Daydream Diary" channel.

What we access

The TikTok account's basic profile (open ID, display name) and permission to upload videos to that account, granted through TikTok Login.

How we use it

Only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. Nothing else.

Storage and sharing

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data, and we do not access other users' data.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the local token file.
